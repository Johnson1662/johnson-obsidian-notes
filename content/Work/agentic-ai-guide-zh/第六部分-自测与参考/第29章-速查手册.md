## 29.1 核心 RL 与对齐公式

$$
\mathrm{PPOClip:} L = \mathbb {E} [ \min (r _ {t} \hat {A} _ {t}, \operatorname{clip} (r _ {t}, 1 \pm \epsilon) \hat {A} _ {t}) ], r _ {t} = \pi_ {\theta} (a _ {t} | s _ {t}) / \pi_ {\mathrm{old}} (a _ {t} | s _ {t})\tag{29.1}
$$

$$
\mathrm{DPO:} L = - \mathbb {E} [ \log \sigma (\beta \log \frac {\pi_ {\theta} (y _ {w} | x)}{\pi_ {\mathrm{ref}} (y _ {w} | x)} - \beta \log \frac {\pi_ {\theta} (y _ {l} | x)}{\pi_ {\mathrm{ref}} (y _ {l} | x)}) ]\tag{29.2}
$$

$$
\mathrm{GRPO:} \quad \hat {A} _ {i} = (r _ {i} - \mu_ {G}) / \sigma_ {G}, \quad \text { then   PPO   clip   update   (no   critic) }\tag{29.3}
$$

$$
\text { KTO: } \quad L = \lambda_ {w} (1 - v (y _ {w})) + \lambda_ {l} \cdot v (y _ {l}), \quad v = \sigma (\beta \log (\pi_ {\theta} / \pi_ {\mathrm{ref}}) - z)\tag{29.4}
$$

$$
\text { IPO: } L = \mathbb {E} [ (\log (\pi_ {\theta} (y _ {w}) / \pi_ {\text { ref}} (y _ {w})) - \log (\pi_ {\theta} (y _ {l}) / \pi_ {\text { ref}} (y _ {l})) - 1 / (2 \beta)) ^ {2} ]\tag{29.5}
$$

$$
\text { ORPO: } \quad L = L _ {\text { SFT }} (y _ {w}) - \lambda \log \sigma (\log (\text { odds } (y _ {w}) / \text { odds } (y _ {l})))\tag{29.6}
$$

$$
\mathrm{GAE:} \quad \hat {A} _ {t} = \sum_ {l = 0} ^ {T - t} (\gamma \lambda) ^ {l} \delta_ {t + l}, \quad \delta_ {t} = r _ {t} + \gamma V (s _ {t + 1}) - V (s _ {t})\tag{29.7}
$$

$$
\mathrm{KLPenalty:} R _ {\mathrm{total}} = r _ {\phi} (x, y) - \beta D _ {\mathrm{KL}} [ \pi_ {\theta} (y | x) \| \pi_ {\mathrm{ref}} (y | x) ]\tag{29.8}
$$

$$
\mathrm{RM} (\text { Bradley - Terry }): L = - \mathbb {E} [ \log \sigma (r _ {\phi} (x, y _ {w}) - r _ {\phi} (x, y _ {l})) ]\tag{29.9}
$$

$$
\mathrm{Best-of-N:} y ^ {*} = \arg \max _ {y _ {i} \sim \pi_ {\theta} (\cdot | x), i = 1.. N} r _ {\phi} (x, y _ {i})\tag{29.10}
$$

## 29.2 Transformer 与架构公式

$$
\text { Self - Attention: } \quad \operatorname{Attn} (Q, K, V) = \operatorname{softmax} (Q K ^ {\top} / \sqrt {d _ {k}}) \cdot V\tag{29.11}
$$

$$
\text { Multi - Head: } \quad \mathrm{MHA} (X) = \mathrm{Concat} (\mathrm{head} _ {1}, \ldots , \mathrm{head} _ {h}) W ^ {O}, \quad \mathrm{head} _ {i} = \mathrm{Attn} (X W _ {i} ^ {Q}, X W _ {i} ^ {K}, X W _ {i} ^ {V})\tag{29.12}
$$

$$
\mathrm{RoPE:} f (x _ {m}, m) = x _ {m} e ^ {i m \theta_ {j}}, \quad \theta_ {j} = 1 0 0 0 0 ^ {- 2 j / d}\tag{29.13}
$$

$$
\text { LoRA: } \quad W ^ {\prime} = W _ {0} + (\alpha / r) \cdot B A, \quad B \in \mathbb {R} ^ {d \times r},   A \in \mathbb {R} ^ {r \times k}\tag{29.14}
$$

$$
\mathrm{KD} (\mathrm{softtargets}): L _ {\mathrm{KD}} = (1 - \alpha) L _ {\mathrm{CE}} (y, \hat {y}) + \alpha T ^ {2} \cdot \mathrm{KL} (p _ {T} ^ {\mathrm{teacher}} \| p _ {T} ^ {\mathrm{student}})\tag{29.15}
$$

$$
\text { FFN   (SwiGLU): } \quad \text { FFN } (x) = (\text { Swish } (x W _ {1}) \odot x W _ {3}) W _ {2}\tag{29.16}
$$

## 29.8 RAG 流水线公式

$$
\text { Cosine   similarity: } \quad \operatorname{sim} (q, d) = \frac {q \cdot d}{\| q \| \cdot \| d \|}\tag{29.17}
$$

$$
\text { Retrieval: } \quad \mathcal {D} _ {k} = \operatorname{top-} k _ {d \in \mathcal {C}}   \operatorname{sim} (\operatorname{embed} (q),   \operatorname{embed} (d))\tag{29.18}
$$

$$
\text { RAG   generation: } \quad P (y | q) = P _ {\mathrm{LLM}} (y \mid q, \mathcal {D} _ {k})\tag{29.19}
$$

$$
\text { Chunking   overlap: } \quad \text { stride } = \text { chunk\_size } - \text { overlap }\tag{29.20}
$$

$$
\text {   Reranker   (cross - enc):   } \quad \text {   score   } (q, d) = \text { MLP(BERT([q;d])) }\tag{29.21}
$$

## 29.11 上下文窗口预算

$$
C \geq \underbrace {S} _ {\text {system}} + \underbrace {M} _ {\text {memory / RAG}} + \underbrace {T} _ {\text {tool defs}} + \underbrace {H} _ {\text {history}} + \underbrace {R} _ {\text {reserved output}}\tag{29.22}
$$

经验法则（针对 128K 上下文）：

- 系统提示：1–4K token（固定）

- 工具定义：2–8K（随工具数量增长）

- RAG 上下文：4–16K（top-k 个 chunk）

- 历史记录：无界增长 → 汇总或截断

- 预留输出：2–8K

## 29.13 方法选择决策树

1. 是否有成对偏好数据（chosen + rejected）？

- 标签噪声大 → IPO

- 显存受限、尚未做 SFT → ORPO

- 数据干净、算力有限 → DPO

- DPO 进入瓶颈、希望加入探索 → Online DPO

2. 只有二值反馈（点赞 / 点踩）？ → KTO

3. 有可验证奖励（数学 / 代码）？ → GRPO

4. 不惜代价追求最高质量？ → PPO

5. 想要免训练的改进？ → Best-of-N

## 29.20 智能体 RL 公式

$$
\mathrm{TrajectoryGRPO:} \quad \hat {A} _ {i} = (R (\tau_ {i}) - \mu_ {G}) / \sigma_ {G}, \quad R (\tau_ {i}) = \sum_ {t} r _ {t} ^ {(\tau_ {i})}\tag{29.23}
$$

$$
